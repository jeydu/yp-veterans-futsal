Delete your account

YP Organiser app · developer: YP Veterans Futsal

YP Organiser is the app for organisers of the YP Veterans Futsal tournament. Organiser accounts are created by a tournament Super Admin. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time.

How to ask

  1. Send a WhatsApp message or call +960 976 3551.
  2. Say "Please delete my YP Organiser account" and give the email address or phone number you sign in with.
  3. We check that the request comes from you, delete the account, and confirm by message within 30 days (usually much sooner).

What is deleted

  • Your organiser sign-in (email address or phone number and password).
  • Your name and role on the organiser list.

What is kept

  • Tournament results, fixtures, news and photos you added stay on the website, because they are the tournament's public record.
  • Copies in our database provider's automatic backups disappear when those backups expire.

Delete data without deleting an account

You can ask for some or all of your data to be deleted without deleting your account. If you registered a team or were named in a registration (team managers and players), you can ask the same way for your registration details, photos and ID card to be deleted. We delete them within 30 days, unless they are still needed to check eligibility for the current tournament season.
