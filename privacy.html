YP Veterans Futsal

Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

This policy covers the YP Veterans Futsal website (ypveterans.com) and the YP Organiser Android app. Both are run by the organisers of the YP Veterans Futsal tournament, Bodufolhudhoo, Maldives ("we").

What we collect

When a team registers on the website, the team gives us:

  • the team name and island;
  • for each official: name and role, and a phone number for the team manager;
  • for each player: name, age and jersey number;
  • a photo and an ID card photo for each player and official, to check eligibility for the veterans tournament.

When an organiser signs in to the YP Organiser app or the web portal, we use their email address or phone number and password to sign them in. Only people a tournament administrator has added can sign in; there is no public sign-up.

When anyone visits the website, we count the visit with a random number kept in the browser, the page and the kind of device (phone, tablet or computer). This tells us nothing about who you are.

What the app collects

The YP Organiser app is for tournament organisers only. It does not collect your location, contacts, files or any other information from your phone. It does not contain advertising or tracking tools. It sends only what an organiser types or chooses in it (such as match scores, fixtures and team details) to our tournament database.

How we use it

  • To run the tournament: check registrations, make fixtures and publish results.
  • To contact a team manager about their registration.
  • Team names, logos, fixtures, scores and match videos are shown publicly on the website. Player photos, ID cards and phone numbers are never shown publicly.

We do not sell personal information or share it with anyone for advertising.

Where it is kept and who can see it

Our data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, and is sent over encrypted connections. Photos and ID cards are kept in private storage that only signed-in organisers with permission to review registrations can open. The website itself is hosted by GitHub Pages.

How long we keep it

Registrations are kept for the tournament season they were made for and while needed to check eligibility in later seasons. Organiser accounts are kept until an administrator removes them.

Your choices

A player, official or organiser can ask us to see, correct or delete their information, or to remove an organiser account, by contacting us below. We will answer within 30 days.

Children

The tournament is for veteran players, and neither the website nor the app is meant for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
